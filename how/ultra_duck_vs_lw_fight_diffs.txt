1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ROOT ARCHITECTURE FLAW
====================================================================================================
The CoreLoneWolf (LW) and CoreDUCK (DUCK) frameworks represent two distinct architectural paradigms:

A. LoneWolf Paradigm: "Tactical Cohesive Compositions"
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In UltrasLW, every fight defines discrete `ArmyComposition` presets (e.g. Default, Stable, Reliable,
Optimized, Pay2Win, Fast). Each composition is an indivisible 4-player tactical unit:
- Synergies, debuffs, passives, and aggro are hand-tuned specifically for that exact combination.
- Gear (Forge weapons, capes, helms, potions, scrolls) and skill rotations are customized precisely
  for each class within that specific composition.
- Crucially: Classes are NOT interchangeable between different comps. (e.g. Dragon of Time in Nulgath
  ONLY exists in the dual-DoT comp `DOT / DOT / LR / LOO`; it NEVER pairs with StoneCrusher or ArchPaladin).

B. DUCK Paradigm: "Generic Constraint Satisfaction (CSP) Pools"
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In UltrasDUCK, auto-assignment was implemented using `DuckSlotRequirement[]` arrays.
The fatal flaw occurred during the porting process:
Instead of defining cohesive team compositions, candidate classes from completely different LW
compositions were dumped into flat, generic candidate arrays (e.g. `Slot 1 = [DoT, AI, VDK, KE, LR]`,
`Slot 2 = [SC, AF]`, `Slot 3 = [AP]`, `Slot 4 = [LOO]`).

The Fatal Consequences of Flat Candidate Pools:
1. Invalid Class Combinations: The CSP solver can assign `Dragon of Time` with `StoneCrusher`, `AP`,
   and `LOO` on Ultra Dage (which instantly wipes the team due to Dage's healing inversion).
2. Broken Synergy Pairs: In Ultra Nulgath, `Dragon of Time` is only viable when paired with a SECOND
   Dragon of Time. Assigning a solo DoT with SC/AP/LOO destroys the kill pace and wipes on Blade/Overdrive.
3. Inappropriate Support Substitutions: `ArchFiend` was added as a candidate in Slot 2 across multiple
   bosses (Darkon, Tyndarius, Drakath, Engineer, Ezrajal, Warden). In 4-player ultras, ArchFiend CANNOT
   replace StoneCrusher's 50% damage reduction, haste, and mana regeneration.
4. Rigid Weapon Constraint Forcing: `RequiredWeaponEnhancement = "Elysium"` was attached to Slot 1 in
   Nulgath and Gramiel, forcing Elysium onto physical classes like `VDK`, `King's Echo`, and `LR`
   which require `Valiance` or `Dauntless`.

2. DEEP DIVE: THE ULTRA DAGE DISASTER (HEALING INVERSION & SELF-DESTRUCTION)
====================================================================================================
Target Files: DUCKScripts/UltrasDUCK/UltraDageDUCK.cs vs UltrasLW/UltraDage_LW.cs

The Ultra Dage Mechanic:
- Dage casts "Noxious Decay" and "Unleashed Doom", which inverts all standard skill-based healing
  and Heal-Over-Time (HoT) effects into fatal self/party true damage.
- Any player who casts a skill heal (e.g. AP Skill 2 Chant of Light, SC Skill 3 Earth's Endless Song,
  LOO Skill 2 Heartbeat, or DoT Skill 3) inflicts immediate lethal damage and causes a wipe.

LoneWolf Implementation (Correct):
- Compositions: `Default (LR/SC/AP/LOO)`, `Stable (KE/SC/AP/LOO)`, `Reliable (VDK/SC/AP/LOO)`.
- Mandatory Weapon Enhancement: `WeaponSpecial.Health_Vamp` on ALL 4 PLAYERS.
  (Auto-attacks trigger Health Vamp, which safely bypasses Dage's skill-healing penalty).
- Mandatory Cape Enhancement: `CapeSpecial.Vainglory`.
- Pruned Heal-less Skill Rotations:
  - ArchPaladin: `{ 3, 1, 4 }` (Skill 2 Chant of Light completely disabled).
  - StoneCrusher: `{ 2, 4, 1 }` (Skill 3 Earth's Endless Song completely disabled).
  - Lord of Order: `{ 3, 1, 4 }` (Skill 2 Heartbeat completely disabled).
  - Legion Revenant: `{ 3, 4, 2, 1 }`.

DUCK Implementation (Completely Broken):
- Current DageSlots in UltraDageDUCK.cs:
  `Slot 1: [ "Dragon of Time", "King's Echo", "Legion Revenant", "Chaos Avenger" ] (Elysium)`
  `Slot 2: [ "StoneCrusher", "Arachnomancer", "LightCaster" ]`
  `Slot 3: [ "ArchPaladin" ]`
  `Slot 4: [ "Lord of Order" ]`
- Catastrophic Errors:
  1. `Dragon of Time` in Slot 1: DoT's entire damage rotation hinges on Skill 3 (Heal/HoT). When DoT
     casts Skill 3, it instantly self-destructs from Noxious Decay.
  2. `Elysium` in Slot 1: Elysium triggers skill-heal procs, killing the user.
  3. `LightCaster` in Slot 2: LightCaster has active skill heals on Skill 2 and 3, wiping the party.
  4. Missing Global `Health_Vamp` Override: `UltraDageDUCK.cs` failed to call `ApplyDageOverrides`
     to enforce Health Vamp on all accounts.

5. DEEP DIVE: THE "ARCHFIEND IN SLOT 2" SYNDROME ACROSS DAILIES & DARKON
====================================================================================================
Across almost all 4-player DUCK scripts (`Ezrajal`, `Warden`, `Engineer`, `Tyndarius`, `Drakath`, `Darkon`),
`ArchFiend` was added into Slot 2 as `CandidateClasses = new[] { "StoneCrusher", "ArchFiend" }`.

Why this is fundamentally incorrect:
1. StoneCrusher's Core Role in 4-Man Ultras:
   - `StoneCrusher` provides 50% party damage reduction, massive haste buffs, continuous party mana
     regen, and critical damage amplification.
2. Where ArchFiend Actually Belongs:
   - In 7-Player Ultras (e.g. `Astral`, `Kathool`, `Deimos`, `Lich Lord`, `The Beast`), `ArchFiend` is
     an excellent 7th player DPS/debuffer alongside the full 6-player core (`LR`, `SC`, `AP`, `LOO`, `VDK`, `Bard`).
   - In 4-Player Ultras, substituting ArchFiend for StoneCrusher leaves the party without mana regen
     and without 50% damage reduction, resulting in team deaths on heavy ultra nukes.
