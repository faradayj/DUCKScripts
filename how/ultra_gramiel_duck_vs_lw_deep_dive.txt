1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & IDENTIFIED DISCREPANCIES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ultra Gramiel features a 2-phase battle:
- Phase 1: Dual Grace Crystal balance & alternating charge taunts
- Phase 2: Gramiel 7-attack nuke cycles (Attack 1 -> 3 -> 5 -> 7 taunts, Liberator, Charge2, ChargeAttack2, Invulnerable)

In UltraGramielDUCK.cs, all role mechanics were hardcoded to player indices:
1. Crystal MapID Assignments (GetAssignedCrystalMapId):
   - Hardcoded Player 1 & 2 -> Crystal A (MapID 2), Player 3 & 4 -> Crystal B (MapID 3).
2. Crystal Taunt Cycles (OwnsTauntCycle):
   - Hardcoded Player 1 & 3 -> Odd cycles, Player 2 & 4 -> Even cycles.
3. Phase 2 Attack Taunts (GetPhaseTwoTauntAttack):
   - Hardcoded Player 1 -> Attack 1, Player 2 -> Attack 3, Player 3 -> Attack 5, Player 4 -> Attack 7.
4. Safeguard Attackers & Crystal Balancer:
   - Safeguard Attacker hardcoded to Player 2 & 3.
   - Crystal Balancer hardcoded to Player 4.
5. Phase 2 Attack 1 Slowdown:
   - ArchPaladin slowdown hardcoded to Player 3.

Because DUCK accounts are dynamically assigned and sorted alphabetically:
- If ArchPaladin was Player 1 alphabetically: AP targeted Crystal A, taunted Attack 1 instead of Attack 5,
  and failed to execute Phase 2 slowdown on DPS taunt!
- If Lord of Order was Player 2 alphabetically: LOO targeted Crystal A instead of Crystal B, and never balanced crystals.
- Result: Crystals shattered the team in Phase 1 or Gramiel wiped the party on Attack 1/3 in Phase 2.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. MATHEMATICAL CLASS-BASED REMEDIATION IN DUCK
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All assignments and taunts will be bound directly to class role booleans:

1. Class Role Flags:
   - `isDPS`: Non-SC, non-AP, non-LOO (e.g. `Verus DoomKnight`, `Shaman`, `Legion Revenant`)
   - `isStoneCrusher`: `preset.ClassName == "StoneCrusher"`
   - `isArchPaladin`: `preset.ClassName == "ArchPaladin"`
   - `isLordOfOrder`: `preset.ClassName == "Lord of Order"`

2. Phase 1 Crystal Assignments:
   - `isDPS || isStoneCrusher`: `CrystalAMapId` (MapID 2)
   - `isArchPaladin || isLordOfOrder`: `CrystalBMapId` (MapID 3)

3. Phase 1 Crystal Taunt Cycles:
   - `isDPS || isArchPaladin`: Odd cycles (1, 3, 5...)
   - `isStoneCrusher || isLordOfOrder`: Even cycles (2, 4, 6...)

4. Phase 1 Roles:
   - Safeguard Attackers: `isStoneCrusher || isArchPaladin`
   - Crystal Balancer: `isLordOfOrder` (maintains HP balance between Crystal A & B)
   - Shaman Skill 4: Casts Skill 4 on Gramiel opening & Safeguard

5. Phase 2 Attack Taunts:
   - `isDPS`: Attack 1
   - `isStoneCrusher`: Attack 3
   - `isArchPaladin`: Attack 5 (restricts rotation to {2} during DPS Attack 1)
   - `isLordOfOrder`: Attack 7

6. Enhancements & Overrides:
   - Shaman: Wizard, Elysium, Absolution, Sage Tonic, Potent Malevolence Elixir
   - Legion Revenant: Penitence Cape, Helm None, Skills { 3, 4, 2 }
   - ArchPaladin: Penitence Cape
   - Lord of Order: Valiance Weapon
   - preset.CombatPotion = null across all taunters
