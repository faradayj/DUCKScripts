1. OVERVIEW & REWARD TOKENOMICS (LINEAGE OF DEVASTATION)
====================================================================================================
The Legion Daily Boss system comprises five distinct challenge encounters designed to award the
coveted endgame AC-tagged crafting resource:
    - Item Name: "Lineage of Devastation"
    - Item Type: AC Drop / Resource
    - Max Stack: 1000

Collecting Lineage of Devastation enables players to craft and unlock pinnacle Legion merge shop items,
such as "Void Vigilante of the Legion", Legion dark caster equipment, and unique cosmetic armor sets.
By running all 5 dailies every day, an army generates 5 Lineage of Devastation per account daily.
