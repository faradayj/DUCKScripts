1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & IDENTIFIED DISCREPANCIES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An in-depth semantic audit of UltraDageDUCK.cs compared to UltraDage_LW.cs revealed that
UltraDageDUCK suffered from player indexing bugs when assigning scroll roles, taunt partnerships,
and packet detectors:

1. HARDCODED PLAYER NUMBER SCROLL AND TAUNT ASSIGNMENTS
   - In UltraDageDUCK.cs:
       isTaunter = Duck.IsArmyPlayer(1) || Duck.IsArmyPlayer(3);
       if (Duck.IsArmyPlayer(2)) Duck.PrepareScrolls(DecayScroll);
       else if (Duck.IsArmyPlayer(1) || Duck.IsArmyPlayer(3)) Duck.PrepareScrolls(EnrageScroll);
       else { Duck.PrepareScrolls(MystifyScroll); Duck.PrepareScrolls(DecayScroll); }
   - In dynamic multi-client setups, accounts are sorted alphabetically:
     * If StoneCrusher is Player 1 alphabetically: SC was marked as `isTaunter`, equipped `Scroll of Enrage`,
       and attempted to alternate focus taunts instead of casting `Scroll of Decay` on Dage's heal window!
     * If Lord of Order was Player 3 alphabetically: LOO equipped `Scroll of Enrage` instead of `Scroll of Mystify`.
     * If ArchPaladin was Player 4 alphabetically: AP was treated as Player 4, equipped `Scroll of Mystify`,
       and never taunted!
     * If DPS was Player 2 alphabetically: DPS equipped `Scroll of Decay` and never taunted Dage!
   - Result: Dage was not taunted, focus auras dropped, Dage's Noxious Decay nuked the team,
     and Dage healed back to full HP continuously.

2. HARDCODED TAUNT PARTNERSHIP RESOLUTION (FightEnrageTaunter)
   - `partnerPlayerNumber` assumed `1` and `3` regardless of which physical player had `DPS` or `ArchPaladin`.
   - Partner dead/alive detection and army signal listening desynced whenever player numbers did not match
     the legacy fixed layout.

3. HARDCODED DECAY PACKET DETECTOR & CASTING
   - In `StartDecayDetector` and `HandleDecayDetections`:
       bool detectsDecay = Duck.IsArmyPlayer(2) || (Duck.IsArmyPlayer(4) && !mystifyMode);
   - If StoneCrusher was Player 1 or 3, SC never started the packet detector and never cast `Scroll of Decay`.

====================================================================================================
2. CORRECT ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN IN DUCK
====================================================================================================
Roles, scrolls, taunt loops, and packet detection must be mapped directly to assigned classes:

1. Class Role Flags:
   - `isArchPaladin`: `preset.ClassName == "ArchPaladin"`
   - `isLordOfOrder`: `preset.ClassName == "Lord of Order"`
   - `isStoneCrusher`: `preset.ClassName == "StoneCrusher"`
   - `isDPS`: Non-AP, non-LOO, non-SC (e.g. `Legion Revenant`, `Chaos Slayer`, `Verus DoomKnight`, `King's Echo`)

2. Scroll Assignments & Taunt Roles:
   - `isTaunter`: `isDPS || isArchPaladin`
   - `isOpeningTauntOwner`: `isDPS` (Taunts at fight start & opens Focus alternation)
   - `ArchPaladin`: Secondary taunter (Alternates Focus taunts with DPS)
   - `StoneCrusher`: Primary Decay holder (Casts `Scroll of Decay` on decay packet detections)
   - `Lord of Order`: Scroll holder (`Scroll of Mystify` or fallback `Scroll of Decay`)
   - `if (isTaunter) preset.CombatPotion = null;`

3. Dynamic Taunt Partner Resolution:
   - `isDPS` resolves `ArchPaladin`'s player index from `assignResult.PlayerNumberToClass`.
   - `ArchPaladin` resolves `DPS`'s player index from `assignResult.PlayerNumberToClass`.

4. Health Vamp & Vainglory Baseline:
   - `preset.WeaponEnhancement = WeaponSpecial.Health_Vamp;`
   - `preset.CapeEnhancement = CapeSpecial.Vainglory;`
   - Direct heals omitted from ordinary skill rotation for all support classes.
