1. ARCHITECTURE & FIXED PLAYER SLOT ROLES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
UltraSpeaker_LW uses a fixed 4-player slot architecture (`LoneWolf.IsArmyPlayer(1..4)`):

  - Player 1 (`LoneWolf.IsArmyPlayer(1)`):
    * In Default / Test / Test3: Legion Revenant (DPS, Section 2 Listen taunter, Opening Truth taunter)
    * In Stable / Test2: Verus DoomKnight (DPS, Section 2 Listen taunter, Opening Truth taunter)
    * In Pay2Win: Guardian (Tank/DPS, Zone Owner 1, does not taunt)
    * Special Duty: Army coordinator (handles sync files, publishes chart reset signals).
  - Player 2 (`LoneWolf.IsArmyPlayer(2)`):
    * StoneCrusher (Support, Section 1 Listen taunter, Zone Owner 1 in Default, Divine Elixir user)
  - Player 3 (`LoneWolf.IsArmyPlayer(3)`):
    * In Default / Stable / Test: ArchPaladin (Lead debuffer/healer, Section 1 Truth taunter, Zone Owner 3)
    * In Pay2Win: Legion Revenant (DPS, Zone Owner 2)
  - Player 4 (`LoneWolf.IsArmyPlayer(4)`):
    * In Default / Stable / Test: Lord of Order (Buffer, Opening Listen taunter, Section 2 Truth taunter, Zone Owner 4)
    * In Pay2Win: ArchPaladin (Debuffer/healer, Zone Owner 3)

2. ARMY COMPOSITIONS SUPPORTED & DIFFERENCES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The script supports 6 distinct `ArmyComposition` configurations (lines 27-35):

1. `Default` (LR / SC / AP / LOO):
   - Standard 4-player meta composition.
   - All 4 players taunt. AP casts Skill 3 on Truth in Section 1 and Section 2. LOO casts Skill 4 on Truth in Section 1.

2. `Stable` (VDK / SC / AP / LOO):
   - Replaces Legion Revenant with Verus DoomKnight for higher survivability and single-target DPS.
   - Same taunt and zone rotation as Default.

3. `Pay2Win` (Guardian / SC / LR / AP):
   - Player 1 is Guardian; Player 2 is SC; Player 3 is LR; Player 4 is AP.
   - Guardian does not taunt (`IsTaunter()` is false for P1).
   - Zone rotation order is `{ 1, 3, 4, 2 }` (Guardian -> LR -> AP -> SC).
   - Dedicated 5-section taunt chart (`Pay2WinOpeningSteps`, `Pay2WinSectionOneSteps`..`Pay2WinSectionFourSteps`).

4. `Test` (LR / SC / AP / LOO - Restricted AP Heals):
   - ArchPaladin starts with `Skills = { 1 }` (Commandment only).
   - AP receives exactly 2 unlocked casts of Skill 2 (Chant of Light) at the start of the fight, plus 2 unlocked casts each time a zone clear signal is received (`ProcessTestHealClearSignals`).

5. `Test2` (VDK / SC / AP / LOO - Absolute Priority Taunts):
   - Uses VDK. Enables `UsesAllFreshAbsolutePriorityTaunts() = true`, meaning EVERY fresh warning (both Truth and Listen) pauses all player skill engines to guarantee zero taunt clipping.

6. `Test3` (LR / SC / AP / LOO - Absolute Priority Taunts):
   - Uses LR. Enables `UsesAllFreshAbsolutePriorityTaunts() = true`.

   - ArchPaladin:
     * In Default / Stable: `preset.Skills = new[] { 2, 1 };` (Skills 3 & 4 removed from auto-loop. Skill 3 is queued ONLY when called by the chart. Skill 4 is queued ONLY when Righteous Seal is expiring).

     * In Default / Stable: `preset.Skills = new[] { 2, 3, 1 };` (Skill 4 removed from auto-loop; queued ONLY when called by the chart on Section 1 Step 3).

5. ZONE MECHANICS & THE EQUALIZE / SANCTITY SYSTEM
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1 Coordinate Layout & Movement:
  - Safe Zone (Green/Safe): `SafeX = 890, SafeY = 395`
  - Red Zone (Equalize/Zone): `RedX = 731, RedY = 380`
  - Coordinate Tolerance: $\pm 20\text{ px}$ (`CoordinateTolerance = 20`)
  - Movement Call: `Bot.Flash.Call("walkTo", x, y, 8)` with 500ms confirmation retry (`MovementRetryDelay = 500`).

5.2 Equalize Event Detection (`OnRunToArea` lines 469-473):
  - Skua listens for client event `RunToArea`.
  - When `zone == "A"`, `Interlocked.Increment(ref equalizeDetectionCount)` increments the thread-safe counter.

5.3 Zone Owner Sequence per Composition (`GetZoneOwner(cycle)`):
  - In Default / Stable / Test (`ZoneOwners = { 2, 1, 3, 4 }`):
    * Cycle 1: Player 2 (StoneCrusher)
    * Cycle 2: Player 1 (Legion Revenant / Verus DoomKnight)
    * Cycle 3: Player 3 (ArchPaladin)
    * Cycle 4: Player 4 (Lord of Order)
    * (Cycles 5+ repeat modulo 4: Cycle 5 -> P2, Cycle 6 -> P1, etc.)
  - In Pay2Win (`Pay2WinZoneOwners = { 1, 3, 4, 2 }`):
    * Cycle 1: Player 1 (Guardian)
    * Cycle 2: Player 3 (Legion Revenant)
    * Cycle 3: Player 4 (ArchPaladin)
    * Cycle 4: Player 2 (StoneCrusher)

5.4 State Machine & Zone Clear Signals (`ProcessZoneState` lines 786-866):
  1. Initial Position:
     - The Cycle 1 Zone Owner (P2) walks to `RedX, RedY` (`731, 380`).
     - The other 3 players walk to `SafeX, SafeY` (`890, 395`).
  2. Equalize Hits:
     - Speaker casts Equalize. The player in `RedX, RedY` takes the mechanic and receives the `Sanctity` aura.
     - `ProcessEqualizeDetections()` validates that the designated owner was at `RedX, RedY`.
     - Owner sets `zoneState.WaitingForSanctityCycle = cycle`.
     - The NEXT owner sets `zoneState.WaitingForClearCycle = cycle`.
  3. Sanctity Resolution & Movement to Safe:
     - The owner detects `Bot.Self.GetAura("Sanctity") != null`.
     - The owner immediately walks to `SafeX, SafeY`.
     - Upon reaching `SafeX, SafeY`, the owner sends army signal `SPEAKER_ZONE_{fightAttempt}_{cycle}_CLEAR`.
  4. Next Owner Enters Red Zone:
     - The next owner receives `SPEAKER_ZONE_{fightAttempt}_{cycle}_CLEAR` from the previous owner.
     - The next owner immediately walks to `RedX, RedY` (`731, 380`) to prepare for the upcoming Equalize.

5.5 Brute Force Mode (`bruteForceMethod`):
  - When enabled, all 4 players stay permanently at `SafeX, SafeY` and ignore Equalize movement entirely.

6. WARNING PACKET DETECTION, TAUNT CHART, & PRIORITY SKILLS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1 Packet Choice Detector (`StartPacketChoiceDetector`):
  - Listens on packet command `"ct"` for two warning strings:
    * `TruthMessage` = `"I will make you see the truth."`
    * `ListenMessage` = `"You shall listen."`
  - When `"You shall listen."` arrives, the packet detector PAUSES normal skill casting on all players to prevent skill animation locks from delaying the mandatory taunt.

6.2 Fresh vs Covered Taunts:
  - **Fresh Taunt (`expected.Fresh == true`)**:
    The designated player MUST cast `Scroll of Enrage` (Skill 5) within the reaction window.
  - **Covered Taunt (`expected.Fresh == false`)**:
    The player who already has aggro from the previous taunt does NOT taunt again (saving scrolls and cooldowns).

6.3 Normal Priority vs Absolute Priority Taunts:
  - **Truth Taunts**: Called via `LoneWolf.RequestTaunt(1)`.
  - **Listen Taunts**: Called via `LoneWolf.RequestAbsolutePriorityTaunt(1)`. This forces an immediate queue wipe and skill priority execution. Non-taunting players call `LoneWolf.ResumeSkillEngine()` to resume normal DPS once the taunt connects.

6.4 Chart-Triggered Priority Skills:
  - **ArchPaladin Skill 3 (Righteous Seal)**:
    Triggered on Section 1 Step 1 (Truth) and Section 2 Step 2 (Truth). Fired via `LoneWolf.RequestPrioritySkill(3)`.
  - **Lord of Order Skill 4 (Quixotic)**:
    Triggered on Section 1 Step 3 (Truth). Fired via `LoneWolf.RequestPrioritySkill(4)` to negate incoming burst.

6.5 ArchPaladin Righteous Seal Monitoring (`MaintainArchPaladinRighteousSeal` lines 1055-1085):
  - In addition to chart triggers, ArchPaladin actively monitors the `Righteous Seal` aura on Speaker (`SpeakerMapId = 1`).
  - When remaining aura duration is $\le 1000\text{ ms}$ (`RighteousSealSkillFourWindow`), ArchPaladin immediately queues **Skill 4** (`LoneWolf.RequestPrioritySkill(4)`) to re-apply the debuff before it falls off.

8. DEATH RECOVERY, CHART FAILURE SIGNALS, RESETS, AND QUEST COMPLETION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Death Recovery (`RestoreZonePositionAfterDeath` lines 908-958):
   - When a player respawns after dying:
     * Jumps back to `Boss, Left`.
     * If the player is NOT the next zone owner, it immediately walks to `SafeX, SafeY`.
     * If the player IS the next zone owner, it checks if the previous owner already sent the zone clear signal. If sent, it walks into `RedX, RedY`; otherwise, it waits at `SafeX, SafeY` until the clear signal arrives.

2. Chart Failure Detection & Broadcast (`ReportChartFailure` & `PublishChartResetIfRequested`):
   - Triggers if:
     * An unexpected warning packet is detected (e.g. Listen when Truth was expected).
     * Equalize hits before all steps in the current section are completed.
     * The designated zone owner was not in `RedX, RedY` when Equalize hit.
   - Flow:
     1. The detecting player sends `SPEAKER_CHART_FAILURE_{fightAttempt}`.
     2. Player 1 (Coordinator) detects the failure signal and broadcasts `SPEAKER_CHART_RESET_{fightAttempt}` to all 4 accounts.
     3. All 4 accounts exit combat, cancel target, jump to `Enter, Spawn`, and sync on `FIGHT_RESET_{fightAttempt}_SAFE`.

3. Quest Completion:
   - Once Speaker reaches 0 HP, jumps to `Enter, Spawn`.
   - Calls `LoneWolf.CompleteUltraQuest(9173)`.
   - Syncs on `FINISH`.
   - Player 1 sleeps 2000ms, calls `LoneWolf.StopArmySync("COMPLETE")`, and stops the army.
