1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & IDENTIFIED DISCREPANCIES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An in-depth semantic audit of UltraNulgathDUCK.cs compared to UltraNulgath_LW.cs revealed that
UltraNulgathDUCK suffered from the exact same dynamic player indexing flaw as UltraDrakathDUCK:

1. HARDCODED PLAYER NUMBER TAUNT & PACKET DETECTOR ASSIGNMENTS
   - In UltraNulgathDUCK.cs:
       isTaunterOne = Duck.IsArmyPlayer(1);
       isTaunter = isTaunterOne || Duck.IsArmyPlayer(4);
       if (Duck.IsArmyPlayer(4) && !Duck.StartPacketDetector(PacketCommand, AbyssPacketText)) ...
       bool playerFour = Duck.IsArmyPlayer(4);
   - In DUCK, players are sorted alphabetically by username:
     * If Lord of Order is Player 1 alphabetically, LOO is wrongly marked as `isTaunterOne`.
     * If ArchPaladin is Player 2 alphabetically, AP is NOT marked as a taunter at all!
     * If StoneCrusher is Player 4 alphabetically, SC starts the packet detector and tries to
       perform the LOO Abyss taunt!
   - Result:
     * ArchPaladin never equips Scroll of Enrage and never performs opening or response taunts.
     * Lord of Order never starts the Abyss packet detector and never sends Abyss taunt signals.
     * Nulgath immediately wipes the party with un-taunted opening fury and unmitigated Abyss nukes.

2. TARGETING LOGIC HARDCODED TO PLAYER INDICES (GetTargetMapId)
   - In UltraNulgathDUCK.cs:
       if (!Duck.IsArmyPlayer(2)) return NulgathMapId;
   - Only `Player 2` (alphabetical) targeted the Overfiend Blade! If Player 2 was not StoneCrusher
     or VDK, no one burned down the Blade, allowing the Blade's damage stacking to wipe the team.

3. TAUNTER POTION SUPPRESSION
   - Taunters (ArchPaladin and Lord of Order) must have `preset.CombatPotion = null` so Slot 5 is
     100% reserved for `Scroll of Enrage` without potion casting contention.

====================================================================================================
2. CORRECT ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN IN DUCK
====================================================================================================
Roles, packet detection, taunts, and target selection must be mapped directly to the assigned class:

1. Roles:
   - `ArchPaladin`: `isTaunterOne = true` (Performs opening taunt @ T+5s and follow-up Abyss taunt).
   - `Lord of Order`: `isAbyssDetector = true` (Starts packet detector, taunts @ T+2s on "Abyss!", broadcasts signal).
   - `StoneCrusher`: Blade Damager (Targets Blade until HP <= 750,000, then attacks Nulgath).
   - `DPS (VDK / KE)`: Helps burst Blade, then burns Nulgath.

2. Combat Potions:
   - `isTaunter = isTaunterOne || isAbyssDetector;`
   - `if (isTaunter) preset.CombatPotion = null;`
   - Non-taunters (SC and DPS) maintain their combat potions (`Potent Honor Potion`, `Felicitous Philtre`).

3. Dynamic Target Map Selection (GetTargetMapId):
   - `StoneCrusher`: Blade (MapID 1) if Blade HP > 750,000, else Nulgath (MapID 2).
   - `Verus DoomKnight`: Blade while alive, else Nulgath.
   - `King's Echo`: Blade while Blade HP > 500,000, else Nulgath.
   - `ArchPaladin` & `Lord of Order`: Nulgath (MapID 2) exclusively.
