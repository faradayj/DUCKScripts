1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & IDENTIFIED DISCREPANCIES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An in-depth code audit of UltraDarkonDUCK.cs compared to UltraDarkon_LW.cs revealed the same
critical player indexing mismatch that broke taunts, heals, and packet detection in other ultras:

1. HARDCODED PLAYER NUMBER TAUNT & PACKET DETECTOR ASSIGNMENTS
   - In UltraDarkonDUCK.cs:
       isTaunter = Duck.IsArmyPlayer(1) || Duck.IsArmyPlayer(2);
       bool detectsAttack2 = UsesDefaultFightRoles() ? Duck.IsArmyPlayer(4) : Duck.IsArmyPlayer(1);
       if (Duck.IsArmyPlayer(4)) ReleaseLooSkillFour(...);
       if (Duck.IsArmyPlayer(4)) TryQueueLooExtraHeal(...);
       private bool IsArchPaladinPlayer() => Duck.IsArmyPlayer(3);
       private bool IsOpeningTauntOwner() => Duck.IsArmyPlayer(1);
   - In DUCK, players are sorted ALPHABETICALLY by username:
     * If Lord of Order is Player 1 alphabetically: LOO was marked as `isTaunter` and `IsOpeningTauntOwner`,
       equipping Scroll of Enrage and taunting Darkon into one-shotting LOO!
     * If ArchPaladin was Player 1 or 2: AP equipped Scroll of Enrage and taunted, interrupting Righteous Seal.
     * If StoneCrusher or DPS was Player 3 or 4: They NEVER taunted, causing Darkon's Attack3 nukes
       to hit un-taunted party members for lethal damage.
     * If Lord of Order was Player 2 or 3: LOO NEVER ran `ReleaseLooSkillFour`, spammed Quixotic early,
       and never ran Attack2 packet detection or timed extra heals.

2. DEFENSIVE ENHANCEMENT MATRIX & VAINGLORY REMEDIATION
   - `ApplyDarkonOverrides` in UltraDarkonDUCK.cs correctly sets:
     * `StoneCrusher`: Cape = `Absolution`
     * `Lord of Order`: Skills = `{ 3, 1 }`, Cape = `Absolution`
     * `ArchPaladin`: Skills = `{ 3, 1, 4 }`, Cape = `Penitence`
     * `DPS`: Cape = `Penitence`
   - However, taunter combat potions were not suppressed on the actual class taunters (`isTaunter`).

====================================================================================================
2. CORRECT ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN IN DUCK
====================================================================================================
Roles, packet markers, and combat mechanics must be mapped directly to assigned classes:

1. Class Role Flags:
   - `isArchPaladin`: `preset.ClassName == "ArchPaladin"`
   - `isLordOfOrder`: `preset.ClassName == "Lord of Order"`
   - `isStoneCrusher`: `preset.ClassName == "StoneCrusher"`
   - `isDPS`: Non-AP, non-LOO, non-SC (e.g. `Legion Revenant`, `Verus DoomKnight`, `LightCaster`, `King's Echo`)

2. Taunt Mechanics:
   - `isTaunter`: `isDPS || isStoneCrusher`
   - `isOpeningTauntOwner`: `isDPS` (Taunts at start & odd Attack3 cycles)
   - `StoneCrusher`: Taunts on even Attack3 cycles
   - `if (isTaunter) preset.CombatPotion = null;`

3. Packet Detection & Heals:
   - `Lord of Order`: Listens for `Attack2Marker`, manages `ReleaseLooSkillFour`, and queues timed heals.
   - `ArchPaladin`: Listens for `Attack3Marker`, manages `HandleArchPaladin` and Phase 3 midpoint heals.
   - `DPS` & `StoneCrusher`: Listen for `Attack3Marker` to trigger interleaved taunts.

4. Elimination of Legacy Methods:
   - Remove unused `GetClassPreset()` and `GetPlayerAlias()` that referenced fixed `Duck.IsArmyPlayer` indices.
