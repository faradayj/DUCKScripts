EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: WHY ULTRAGRAMIELDUCK.CS WAS NOT BEATING THE BOSS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The forensic comparison identified 6 critical mechanics in UltraGramiel_LW.cs that were broken,
desynchronized, or hardcoded in UltraGramielDUCK.cs:

1. BROKEN SAFEGUARD ATTACKERS IN PHASE 1 (SHAMAN COMP)
   - In LW: When running Shaman (`Optimized`), ALL 4 PLAYERS (`safeguardAttacker = true`) target Gramiel
     to burst through the Safeguard shield before Gramiel charges.
   - In DUCK: `safeguardAttacker` was hardcoded to `isStoneCrusher || isArchPaladin`. Shaman, VDK, and LOO
     never attacked Safeguard, allowing Gramiel's shield to linger and trigger lethal room wipes.

2. BROKEN CRYSTAL BALANCING IN PHASE 1 (SHAMAN COMP)
   - In LW: In Shaman comp, Player 1 (Shaman) and Player 2 (StoneCrusher) are the designated balancers.
     Because Shaman runs Elysium and deals massive multi-target damage to Crystal A, Shaman MUST switch
     targets to Crystal B whenever HP(A) and HP(B) differ by > 20 HP.
   - In DUCK: `crystalBalancer` was hardcoded to `isLordOfOrder`. Shaman never balanced Crystal B, causing
     Crystal A to melt to 0 HP while Crystal B was still at ~80% HP. When one crystal dies prematurely,
     the remaining crystal enrages and wipes the entire party.

3. MISSING SAFEGUARD STATE ON SHAMAN PRIORITY SKILL 4
   - In LW: Shaman queues Skill 4 on Gramiel at fight opening AND whenever `safeguardActive` is true.
   - In DUCK: Because Shaman was excluded from `safeguardAttacker`, `safeguardActive` was never updated
     on Shaman, completely disabling Shaman's priority Skill 4 casts during Safeguard.

4. PHASE 2 ARCHPALADIN ROTATION RESTRICTION DESYNC
   - In LW: ArchPaladin restricts rotation to `{ 2 }` on Attack 1 ONLY during Legion Revenant comp
     (`UsesDefaultCompositionBehavior()`). In Shaman comp, AP does NOT restrict to `{ 2 }`.
   - In DUCK: ArchPaladin always restricted rotation to `{ 2 }` on Attack 1 regardless of comp.

5. PHASE 2 TAUNT & STATE TRANSITION MAPPINGS
   - In LW:
     * Shaman (P1): Taunts Attack 1 -> stays in `Attacks` state -> disables Skill 3 on Attack 3 -> re-enables Skill 3 on Attack 7 -> transitions to `ChargeTwo`.
     * StoneCrusher (P2): Taunts Attack 3 -> transitions directly to `ChargeTwo`.
     * ArchPaladin (P3): Taunts Attack 5 -> transitions to `Liberator` -> detects Liberator -> transitions to `ChargeTwo`.
     * Lord of Order (P4): Taunts Attack 7 -> transitions to `ChargeTwo`.
   - In DUCK: State transitions were conflated across generic `isDPS` and `isTaunter` flags rather than
     following the precise role state machine.

6. DYNAMIC PLAYER INDEX RESOLUTION
   - DUCK assigns slots alphabetically by username. Without `ResolveRolePlayerNumbers()`, references
     to player roles and taunt sequences could misalign across room accounts.
