1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & IDENTIFIED DISCREPANCIES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ultra Drago requires two pairs of players to manage alternating taunts on two separate guards:
- Guard Pair A (Algie - MapID 3): DPS (Verus DoomKnight / LR) + Lord of Order
  * Signal: ALGIE_TAUNT_{attempt}_{seq}
  * Opening Owner: DPS
  * Partner: Lord of Order
- Guard Pair B (Dene - MapID 1): ArchPaladin + StoneCrusher
  * Signal: DENIE_TAUNT_{attempt}_{seq}
  * Opening Owner: ArchPaladin
  * Partner: StoneCrusher

In UltraDragoDUCK.cs, all pair definitions, signal prefixes, partner numbers, and target lookups
were HARDCODED to player indices:
1. `GetAssignedGuardMapId()`: Hardcoded Player 1 & 4 -> Algie, Player 2 & 3 -> Dene.
2. `GetPartnerPlayerNumber()`: Hardcoded 1 <-> 4, 3 <-> 2.
3. `IsOpeningOwner()`: Hardcoded Player 1 & 3 as opening taunters.
4. `GetTauntSignalPrefix()`: Hardcoded Player 1 & 4 to Algie prefix, Player 2 & 3 to Dene prefix.
5. `GetInitialTarget()` & `GetNormalTarget()`: Hardcoded Player 3 to Dene, everyone else to Algie.
6. Partner Name Lookup: Attempted to call non-existent `GetSetupOption<string>($"player{partnerPlayerNumber}")`.

Because DUCK accounts are dynamically assigned and sorted alphabetically:
- If ArchPaladin was Player 1: AP was assigned Algie instead of Dene, partnered with Player 4,
  and sent ALGIE_TAUNT signals while trying to taunt Dene!
- If StoneCrusher was Player 4: SC was assigned Algie instead of Dene, desyncing both guard loops!
- Result: Guards enraged with unbound Focus, nuking the team within 5 seconds of fight start!

2. MATHEMATICAL CLASS-BASED REMEDIATION IN DUCK
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All pair roles and partner numbers are dynamically resolved at runtime from `assignResult.PlayerNumberToClass`:

1. Dynamic Role Numbers:
   - `dpsPlayerNumber`: Player index for DPS
   - `scPlayerNumber`: Player index for StoneCrusher
   - `apPlayerNumber`: Player index for ArchPaladin
   - `looPlayerNumber`: Player index for Lord of Order

2. Dynamic Guard & Partner Mapping:
   - `isDPS`:
     * Guard: `AlgieMapId` (3)
     * Partner Player Number: `looPlayerNumber`
     * Opening Owner: `true`
     * Signal Prefix: `ALGIE_TAUNT_`
     * Target: `AlgieMapId` (or Drago if dead)
   - `isLordOfOrder`:
     * Guard: `AlgieMapId` (3)
     * Partner Player Number: `dpsPlayerNumber`
     * Opening Owner: `false`
     * Signal Prefix: `ALGIE_TAUNT_`
     * Target: `AlgieMapId` (or Drago if dead)
   - `isArchPaladin`:
     * Guard: `DeneMapId` (1)
     * Partner Player Number: `scPlayerNumber`
     * Opening Owner: `true`
     * Signal Prefix: `DENIE_TAUNT_`
     * Target: `DeneMapId` (or Algie/Drago if dead)
   - `isStoneCrusher`:
     * Guard: `DeneMapId` (1)
     * Partner Player Number: `apPlayerNumber`
     * Opening Owner: `false`
     * Signal Prefix: `DENIE_TAUNT_`
     * Target: `DeneMapId` (or Algie/Drago if dead)

3. Dynamic Partner Name Lookup:
   - Queries `currentAssignResult.DiscoveredPlayers[partnerPlayerNumber - 1]` for real-time player state.

4. Enhancement Overrides & Potion Suppression:
   - `preset.CapeEnhancement = CapeSpecial.Penitence` on AP, SC, LR.
   - `preset.CombatPotion = null` on all taunters.
