1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & IDENTIFIED CRITICAL DISCREPANCIES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ultra Speaker is by far the most timing-critical boss in AQW, requiring exact synchronization of:
1. Warning taunts ("Truth" and "Listen")
2. Chart-timed skill activations (AP Skill 3 Righteous Seal & LOO Skill 4 Quixotic)
3. Equalize zone rotations (Red Zone 731, 380 vs Safe Zone 890, 395)
4. Righteous Seal uptime maintenance (AP Skill 4 within 1s of Seal expiration)

In UltraSpeakerDUCK.cs, every single chart step, taunt owner, priority skill owner, and zone owner
was HARDCODED to player indices 1, 2, 3, and 4:
  OpeningSteps: [ Truth: Player 1, Listen: Player 4 ]
  SectionOneSteps: [ Truth: Player 3 (AP Skill 3), Listen: Player 2, Truth: Player 2 (LOO Skill 4) ]
  SectionTwoSteps: [ Listen: Player 1, Truth: Player 1 (AP Skill 3), Truth: Player 4, Listen: Player 4 ]
  ZoneOwners: [ Player 2, Player 1, Player 3, Player 4 ]

Because DUCK sorts accounts alphabetically:
- If ArchPaladin is Player 1 alphabetically: AP is marked as Player 1, taunts Opening Truth and Section 2 Listen,
  and moves into the Red Zone on Cycle 2 instead of Cycle 3!
- If StoneCrusher is Player 3 alphabetically: SC is marked as Player 3, taunts Section 1 Truth, attempts to cast
  AP's Skill 3 (which SC doesn't have), and enters the Red Zone on Cycle 3 instead of Cycle 1!
- Result: Party wiped within 10-15 seconds on every single attempt!

2. MATHEMATICAL REMEDIATION DESIGN IN DUCK
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All taunt chart steps, skill activations, and zone owners will be dynamically computed at runtime
from the resolved class assignments:

1. Dynamic Player Mapping:
   - `dpsPlayerNumber`: Player index assigned to DPS (VDK / LR)
   - `scPlayerNumber`: Player index assigned to StoneCrusher
   - `apPlayerNumber`: Player index assigned to ArchPaladin
   - `looPlayerNumber`: Player index assigned to Lord of Order

2. Dynamic Chart Step Construction:
   - `OpeningSteps`:
     * Step 0: Truth -> Owner: `dpsPlayerNumber` (fresh: true)
     * Step 1: Listen -> Owner: `looPlayerNumber` (fresh: true)
   - `SectionOneSteps` (Equalize Cycle 1 & 3):
     * Step 0: Truth -> Owner: `apPlayerNumber` (fresh: true, skillOwner: `apPlayerNumber`, skill: 3 [Righteous Seal])
     * Step 1: Listen -> Owner: `scPlayerNumber` (fresh: true)
     * Step 2: Truth -> Owner: `scPlayerNumber` (fresh: false, skillOwner: `looPlayerNumber`, skill: 4 [Quixotic])
   - `SectionTwoSteps` (Equalize Cycle 2 & 4):
     * Step 0: Listen -> Owner: `dpsPlayerNumber` (fresh: true)
     * Step 1: Truth -> Owner: `dpsPlayerNumber` (fresh: false, skillOwner: `apPlayerNumber`, skill: 3 [Righteous Seal])
     * Step 2: Truth -> Owner: `looPlayerNumber` (fresh: true)
     * Step 3: Listen -> Owner: `looPlayerNumber` (fresh: false)
   - `ZoneOwners`:
     `[ scPlayerNumber, dpsPlayerNumber, apPlayerNumber, looPlayerNumber ]`
     * Cycle 1: `scPlayerNumber` (StoneCrusher)
     * Cycle 2: `dpsPlayerNumber` (DPS)
     * Cycle 3: `apPlayerNumber` (ArchPaladin)
     * Cycle 4: `looPlayerNumber` (Lord of Order)

3. Dynamic ArchPaladin Righteous Seal Maintenance:
   - `MaintainArchPaladinRighteousSeal`: Checks `isArchPaladin` and queues Skill 4 within 1000ms of Seal expiration.

4. Class Skill and Enhancement Overrides:
   - `Penitence` cape enhancement on all classes (`CapeSpecial.Penitence`).
   - `HelmSpecial.None` on `Legion Revenant`.
   - `Divine Elixir` on `StoneCrusher`.
   - `ArchPaladin` Skills: `{ 2, 1 }` (omits Skill 3 so it only casts on chart command, omits Skill 4 so it only casts for Seal maintenance).
   - `Lord of Order` Skills: `{ 2, 3, 1 }` (omits Skill 4 Quixotic so it only casts on chart command).
   - `preset.CombatPotion = null` on all taunters to prevent Slot 5 collision with Scroll of Enrage.
