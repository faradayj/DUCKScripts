1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VoidDailies coordinates two major daily quests in AQW:
  1. Quest 9091: "Wrong Turn at Voidbuquerque" (Daily Nulgath Resource Farm)
  2. Quest 8653: "The Encroaching Shadows" (Daily Shadow/Void Insignia Farm)

IMPORTANT NOTE ON VOID NERFKITTEN:
  - Void Nerfkitten is NOT a requirement for Quest 9091 (Wrong Turn at Voidbuquerque).
  - Quest 9091 only requires 3 Void Boss Essences: Nightbane, Xyfrag, and Flibbitiestgibbet.
  - Void Nerfkitten has its own separate quest (Quest 9415: "Nerfkitten's Fancy").

  The army executes the shared bosses in lockstep:

    - Target: Void Xyfrag
    - Objective: Obtain 1x "Xyfrag's ??? Essence" (Quest 9091).
    - Mechanics: 7-player auto-assignment, slime stack cleansing.

  Step 2: Void Flibbitiestgibbet (/join voidflibbi)
    - Target: Void Flibbitiestgibbet
    - Objective: Obtain 1x "Flibbitiestgibbet's ??? Essence" (Quest 9091)
                 AND 1x "Flibbitigiblets" (Quest 8653).
    - Dual drop pickup: Both quest items drop simultaneously upon defeat.

  Step 3: Void Nightbane (/join voidnightbane)
    - Target: Void Nightbane
    - Objective: Obtain 1x "Nightbane's ??? Essence" (Quest 9091).
    - Mechanics: 7-player auto-assignment, rapid burst / taunt rotation.

  Step 4: Warlord Icewing (/join icewing)
    - Target: Warlord Icewing
    - Objective: Obtain 1x "Glacial Pinion" (Quest 8653).
    - Mechanics: Multi-player nuke / high physical damage mitigation.

STAGE 2: INDEPENDENT / SOLO FARM PHASE
  Once the 4 synchronized group bosses are defeated, the army transitions to the independent solo phase:

  Step 5: Hydra Challenge (/join hydrachallenge-9999999)
    - Target: Hydra Head 90
    - Objective: Farm 3x "Hydra Eyeball" (Quest 8653).
    - Class: 100% Dodge / Solo Class (e.g., Yami no Ronin, Chrono, or high-sustain solo).
    - Independent: Accounts farm their own private hydrachallenge room without blocking others.
